# Study on MOSFET Gate Dielectrics: New Trends and Requirements

Academic study on **MOSFET gate dielectric materials**, with emphasis on the limitations of conventional SiO₂, the use of **high-k dielectric materials**, and their impact on the electrical performance of modern MOSFET and SOI FinFET devices.

The work was conducted at the **School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Technical University of Crete** as part of the course **Electronic – Electrotechnical Materials (ΗΡΥ 211)**.

## Overview

As MOSFET dimensions continue to scale down, conventional SiO₂ gate dielectrics encounter significant limitations, particularly increased gate leakage current and short-channel effects.

This study examines the use of **high-k dielectric materials** as an alternative gate dielectric technology and investigates their influence on MOSFET performance.

The report covers both fundamental MOSFET operation and the effects of different dielectric materials on nanoscale transistor characteristics.

## High-k Gate Dielectrics

The report investigates how replacing conventional SiO₂ with materials having a higher dielectric constant can allow increased physical gate-insulator thickness while maintaining a small equivalent oxide thickness.

This approach can help reduce tunneling and gate leakage while supporting continued transistor scaling.

Particular attention is given to **HfO₂**, which demonstrates strong performance as a high-k gate dielectric in the SOI FinFET studies examined in the report.
